A row category is $L_q,M_q,T_q$ in the three boundary windows, and
$B_{i,p,e}$ in bulk word $i$.  Here
$q=1,\ldots,20$ on $L$, $q=-20,\ldots,20$ on $M$, and
$q=-20,\ldots,0$ on $T$; also $i\in\{1,2\}$,
$p\in\{0,\ldots,47\}$, and $e\in\{0,1\}$.  For a bulk position,
$p=(j-\phi_i-16s)\bmod48$, $j_0$ is the unique representative in $[1,48]$
with $j_0\equiv j\pmod{48}$, and
$e=((j-j_0)/48)\bmod2$.  Order the categories as
$L,M,T,B_1,B_2$, numerically within each family; this is the order used by both
sign strings.  Expand each hexadecimal string to binary, retain exactly the first
$\ell$ bits (including leading zeroes), and read $1$ as $+1$ and $0$ as $-1$.
The columns $C,D$ are the signs of $\mu_j$ and $|w_j|$ from
Lemma~\ref{lem:exact}.  A displayed switch $L_q:a$ or $L_q:b$ changes that unique
row from the default mode $c$.  For
